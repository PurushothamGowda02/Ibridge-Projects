# 🤖 AI Chatbot - Quick Start Guide

## ✅ Chatbot Successfully Added!

An AI Learning Assistant chatbot has been integrated into your LMS sidebar.

---

## 🎯 How to Access

### Step 1: Open Your Browser
Go to: **http://localhost:5173**

### Step 2: Login
- Use your student credentials
- Complete eligibility test if needed

### Step 3: Click Chatbot Button
- Look at the **left sidebar**
- Find **"AI Chatbot"** button (💬 icon)
- Click it to open the chat

### Step 4: Start Chatting!
- Type your message
- Press **Enter** or click **Send**
- Get instant responses!

---

## 💬 What Can the Chatbot Do?

### Ask About:
- ✅ **Course recommendations** - "What courses should I take?"
- ✅ **Learning tips** - "How do I track my progress?"
- ✅ **Platform help** - "How do I enroll in a course?"
- ✅ **Quiz information** - "Tell me about quizzes"
- ✅ **AI Tutor features** - "What is AI Tutor Lab?"

### Example Conversations:

**Getting Started:**
```
You: "I'm new here, where do I start?"
Bot: "Welcome! Browse courses in the Library, enroll in 
      a beginner course, and start learning at your pace!"
```

**Course Help:**
```
You: "Can you recommend some courses?"
Bot: "I recommend:
      • Web Development Fundamentals
      • Data Science Basics
      • Python Programming"
```

**Progress Tracking:**
```
You: "How do I track my progress?"
Bot: "Check 'My Learning' section for:
      • Completed lessons
      • Quiz scores
      • Course completion %
      • Learning streak"
```

## 🎨 Chatbot Features

### Interface:
- ✅ **Modern chat UI** - Clean, professional design
- ✅ **Message history** - See all your conversations
- ✅ **Timestamps** - Know when messages were sent
- ✅ **User/Bot avatars** - Easy to distinguish messages
- ✅ **Typing indicator** - See when bot is thinking

### Controls:
- ✅ **Minimize** - Collapse chat to save space
- ✅ **Maximize** - Expand chat back
- ✅ **Clear Chat** - Start fresh conversation
- ✅ **Enter to Send** - Quick keyboard shortcut
